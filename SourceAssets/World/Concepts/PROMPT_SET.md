# World Concept Prompt Set

All concepts were generated in reference-image mode. Each subject clause below was appended to the shared
contract, with an already accepted building or modular prop supplied as the style reference. Depletion and
destroyed states used their accepted complete state as the edit reference.

## Shared Contract

> Ashes of Rum stylized low-poly geometry, hand-painted grounded PBR, warm dry Anatolian palette, high
> three-quarter RTS camera, soft neutral studio ground and background, and a chunky gameplay-readable
> silhouette. Use compact maintained beylik-inspired timber, cream felt, rope lashings, restrained teal
> textile, dark stone, and sun-bleached vegetation as appropriate. Show only the requested isolated asset
> or explicitly counted modular variants. Keep every element fully visible and separated. No people,
> animals, flags, labels, writing, emblems, scenery, magical effects, or detached clutter unless the
> requested asset is itself a debris set.

Numeric dimensions from `GENERATION_MANIFEST.md` were included in every relevant subject clause beside a
1.8 m human reference. They are production targets, not dimensions inferred from image framing.

## Building Clauses

- Hisar complete: compact maintained command pavilion, not a palace, keep, conventional house, poor
  shelter, or disorganized camp; obvious front and attached functional supply canopy only.
- Hisar destroyed: same footprint collapsed inward below the declared height; folded felt and broken
  posts form a low settled silhouette rather than an explosive debris plume.
- House: one modest tent-like dwelling in foundation, visibly half-built, complete, and compact rubble
  states.
- Storehouse: one open timber-and-felt supply shelter in foundation, visibly half-built, complete, and
  compact rubble states; only attached functional sacks, timber, and chests in the complete state.
- Watchtower: one narrow timber tower with fixed compact bolt thrower and no crew, plus foundation,
  visibly half-built, and compact rubble states.

## Supply Clauses

- Two tied gold-brown sack variants; one tied tool bundle; one tied six-log timber bundle; one practical
  closed trade chest.
- Full cache: one organized compact cluster using all four modular supply types with four accessible sides.
- Low cache: preserve layout with roughly one quarter of supplies remaining and strong negative space.
- Empty cache: open empty chest, flattened sackcloth, rope ties, and a small bare pallet only; exhausted,
  not destroyed.

## Battlefield Clauses

- Three top-down seamless square swatches: compact dusty soil, worn packed-earth road, sparse dry-grass
  blend.
- Three small dark-rock clusters; two large route-splitting dark-rock formations.
- Three dry-scrub clumps; three sun-bleached grass clumps; two narrow wind-shaped highland pines.
- Two straight ruined-wall modules; one corner plus one broken end; two low fallen-masonry piles.
- One compact broken caravan cart; four separated debris props: wheel, axle, tied cloth roll, crate.
- Two plain roadside stone markers with no writing or symbols.

3.6 x 3.6 x 2.6 m House, a 3.8 x 3.8 x 2.8 m Storehouse, and a 3.0 m-base x 4.0 m-tall Watchtower on one
