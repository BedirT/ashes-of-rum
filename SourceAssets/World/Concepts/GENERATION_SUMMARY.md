# World Concept Generation Summary

This is an art-direction summary reconstructed from the accepted outputs and the working session. It is
not an exact prompt log. The generation session did not preserve a reliable per-output mapping of full
prompt text, reference-image inputs, rejected attempts, or random generation state, so this file must not
be used as provenance for bit-for-bit reproduction.

The checked-in PNGs are the approved source references. `GENERATION_MANIFEST.md` is authoritative for
inventory and intended world-space targets. Future model intake must measure geometry in Meshy cleanup and
Unity rather than infer dimensions from concept framing.

## Shared Art Direction

- Ashes of Rum stylized low-poly geometry and hand-painted grounded PBR.
- Warm dry Anatolian palette, high three-quarter RTS camera, soft neutral background, and chunky
  gameplay-readable silhouettes.
- Compact maintained beylik-inspired timber, cream felt, rope lashings, restrained teal textile, dark
  stone, and sun-bleached vegetation where appropriate.
- Only the requested isolated asset or explicitly counted modular variants. No people, animals, flags,
  labels, writing, emblems, scenery, or magical effects. Detached parts appear only in declared debris
  sets.

## Accepted Subject Directions

### Buildings

- Hisar: maintained command pavilion rather than a palace, keep, conventional house, poor shelter, or
  disorganized camp. The destroyed state collapses inward into a low settled silhouette.
- House: modest tent-like dwelling with distinct foundation, half-built, complete, and compact-rubble
  states.
- Storehouse: open timber-and-felt supply shelter with distinct construction states and compact rubble;
  its complete state includes only attached functional storage contents.
- Watchtower: narrow timber tower with fixed compact bolt thrower and no crew, plus foundation,
  half-built, and rubble states.

### Supplies

- Two tied sack variants, one tied tool bundle, one tied six-log timber bundle, and one practical trade
  chest.
- Full, low, and exhausted cache states share an organized footprint while reducing visible contents.

### Battlefield

- Three surface swatches: compact dusty soil, worn packed-earth road, and sparse dry-grass blend.
- Small and large dark-rock sets, dry scrub, sun-bleached grass, and narrow wind-shaped highland pines.
- Modular ruined walls and masonry piles, one broken caravan cart, separated caravan-debris props, and
  two plain roadside markers without writing or symbols.
